COMPANY POLICY - ACCOUNTS PAYABLE
---------------------------------
1. High-Value Invoices: Any invoice with an amount above 100,000 requires human approval before entry into the system.
2. Vendor Names: Vendor names entered into the system must match our approved vendor list exactly.
3. Date Formats: All due dates must be entered in the system strictly as YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Missing Information: If a required field (Vendor, Amount, Due Date) is missing from the document, ask the requester. Never guess.
